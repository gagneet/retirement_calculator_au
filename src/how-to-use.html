Monte Carlo simulation uses random variables to model market uncertainty. Each run produces different results by design - this shows the range of possible outcomes. The key metrics to focus on are the success rate and median results across all simulations.

The calculator uses current 2025 Age Pension rates and applies both asset and income tests correctly. However, pension rules can change over time, and individual circumstances may affect eligibility. Always verify with Services Australia or a qualified advisor.
