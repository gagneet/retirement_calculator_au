⚠️ Key overseas retirement rules (March 2026)

• 6-week rule: After 6 weeks continuously overseas the Pension Supplement drops to basic rate only and Energy Supplement stops. Pensioner Concession Card is cancelled. Base pension rate is unchanged.

• 26-week rule: After 26 weeks, an AWLR-proportional rate applies if your Australian Working Life Residence is below 35 years. 35+ years = full rate continues.

• Permanent move: Pension Supplement top-up and Energy Supplement stop from the departure date — not after 6 weeks. Critical distinction.

• Principal home — asset test: The home is fully exempt. If you sell and rent, you become a non-homeowner; higher thresholds apply but sale proceeds are assessable for 24 months unless reinvested.

• Return to AU: Leaving again within 2 years of returning and starting Age Pension may stop payments (exceptions: SSA countries, humanitarian visas).

• Healthcare: Medicare does not cover overseas treatment. Private international health insurance strongly recommended.

• Super: Access rules (age 60+) apply regardless of residency. Some funds close accounts for permanent overseas residents — check with your fund.

• Tax: Foreign residents lose the tax-free threshold and pay 30% on AU-sourced income from the first dollar. Double Tax Agreements may reduce this for SSA countries.
