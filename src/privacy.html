Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2024

Data Collection and Usage

The Australian Retirement Calculator is designed with privacy in mind. All calculations are performed locally in your browser, and no personal financial data is transmitted to our servers.

Information We Collect

  • Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (page views, session duration, geographic region)
  • Local Storage: Your calculator inputs may be saved in your browser's local storage for convenience
  • Technical Data: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps)

Data Security

Your financial information never leaves your device. All calculations are performed client-side using JavaScript. We do not store, process, or transmit any of your personal financial data.

Cookies and Tracking

We use Google Analytics cookies to improve our service. These cookies help us understand usage patterns and improve the calculator's functionality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the feedback mechanisms on our main calculator page.
