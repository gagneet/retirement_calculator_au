i Medicare Levy Surcharge (MLS) The MLS is a SEPARATE charge on top of the standard 2% Medicare Levy. It applies only to high earners WITHOUT private hospital cover:

• $93,001–$108,000: +1.0% of income
• $108,001–$144,000: +1.25% of income
• $144,001+: +1.5% of income

Example: Income $120k without cover → MLS = $1,500/yr extra tax.
If you have private hospital cover (any level), the MLS does not apply.
Note: The standard 2% Medicare Levy applies regardless of private cover.

Without private hospital cover, high earners pay the Medicare Levy Surcharge (1%–1.5% of income above $93,000).

⚠️ Key Overseas Retirement Rules (March 2026)

• Age Pension — 6-week rule: After 6 weeks continuously overseas, the Pension Supplement drops to the basic rate only and the Energy Supplement stops. The Pensioner Concession Card is cancelled. The base pension rate is unchanged at this point.

• Age Pension — 26-week rule: After 26 weeks, an AWLR-proportional rate applies if your Australian Working Life Residence is below 35 years. If 35+ years, the full rate continues.

• Permanent move: If moving to live overseas (not just visiting), the Pension Supplement top-up and Energy Supplement stop from your departure date — not after 6 weeks. This is a critical distinction.

• Principal home — asset test: Services Australia distinguishes homeowner and non-homeowner thresholds (March 2026 verified: homeowner full-pension limit $321,500 single / $481,500 couple; non-homeowner thresholds are $258,000 higher, giving $579,500 single / $739,500 couple). The principal home is fully exempt — it does not appear in the assets test at all. If you sell and rent, you become a non-homeowner and the higher thresholds apply (but sale proceeds are assessable for 24 months unless reinvested in a new home).

• Return to Australia: If you leave again within 2 years of returning and starting Age Pension, payments may stop (exceptions: agreement countries, humanitarian visas).

• Healthcare: Medicare does not cover overseas treatment. Private international health insurance is strongly recommended. Pensioner Concession Card expires after 6 weeks abroad.

• Superannuation: Access rules (age 60+) apply regardless of residency. Some funds close accounts for permanent overseas residents — check with your fund.

• Tax: Foreign residents lose the tax-free threshold and pay 30% on Australian-sourced income from the first dollar. Double Tax Agreements may reduce this for agreement countries. Always seek specialist advice.

Aged Care Accommodation: RAD vs DAP

When entering a residential aged care facility, you can pay for your room as a lump sum RAD (Refundable Accommodation Deposit — refunded to your estate on exit) or as an ongoing daily charge DAP (Daily Accommodation Payment), or a combination.

How it works: The RAD is fully refundable — your estate gets it back when you leave the facility. The DAP is an ongoing daily charge calculated using the government's Maximum Permissible Interest Rate (MPIR, currently 7.21% pa). Paying the RAD in full avoids the DAP stream, but you lose the investment returns you could have earned on that money. If your expected portfolio return exceeds the MPIR, you may be better off paying DAP and investing the RAD funds.
About these projections: Figures are inflation-adjusted using category-specific rates (healthcare 6.5%, utilities 5%, general 2.9%). Aged care fees are based on 2024-25 government rates and include the basic daily fee (~$61/day), an average means-tested care fee, and a lifestyle extras package. Actual costs will vary by facility, care needs, and individual financial assessment. Home maintenance assumes $8,000/year for rates, insurance, and upkeep on a paid-off home. Travel and hobbies use your inputs from the calculator above.

Accumulation phase (pre-retirement): Each year, contributions are added and compound growth is applied: Balance × (1 + return) + contribution. Salary sacrifice and employer SG contributions are included.

Decumulation phase (post-retirement): Annual withdrawals are deducted and remaining balance continues to earn assumed returns: Balance × (1 + return) − withdrawal. Age Pension supplements withdrawals once eligible (age 67).

Monte Carlo simulation: When run, the model simulates 1,000+ scenarios with varying return sequences. The success rate shows what proportion of simulations reach your planning horizon with a positive balance.

All results are illustrative. They do not account for individual taxation on investment earnings, contribution tax complexity, means testing nuance, healthcare costs, aged care, or legislative changes. Actual outcomes may differ materially.
