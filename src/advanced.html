💼 Super Strategy (Optional)

i Salary Sacrifice / Additional Concessional Contributions Pre-tax amount you contribute to super on top of the employer Super Guarantee (12%). Salary sacrifice reduces your taxable income — you pay 15% contributions tax instead of your marginal rate.

2024-25 concessional cap: $30,000/year (includes employer SG + salary sacrifice).
At salary $100k: employer SG = $12k. You can sacrifice up to $18k more.

Division 293: If your income + concessional contributions > $250k, an extra 15% tax applies to the excess.
Example: $100k salary → $12k SG → sacrifice $10k → taxable income drops to $90k, saving ~$3.2k in tax

Healthcare & Aged Care

i Medicare Levy Surcharge (MLS) The MLS is a SEPARATE charge on top of the standard 2% Medicare Levy. It applies only to high earners WITHOUT private hospital cover:

• $93,001–$108,000: +1.0% of income
• $108,001–$144,000: +1.25% of income
• $144,001+: +1.5% of income

Example: Income $120k without cover → MLS = $1,500/yr extra tax.
If you have private hospital cover (any level), the MLS does not apply.
Note: The standard 2% Medicare Levy applies regardless of private cover.

Without private hospital cover, high earners pay the Medicare Levy Surcharge (1%–1.5% of income above $93,000).

If you first got hospital cover after age 30, the LHC loading adds 2% per delayed year to your premium. Cleared after 10 years continuous cover.

Affects projected healthcare cost trajectory and aged care probability. Fair/Poor health increases costs and reduces aged care start age.

i Aged Care Probability (%) Likelihood you'll need residential aged care. Australian statistics show ~70% of people will need aged care at some point. Consider family history, health, and gender (women have higher probability due to longer life expectancy).
Example: 68% means 68% chance of needing aged care costing ~$75k/year
i Expected Aged Care Start Age Average age when residential aged care begins. Australian average is around 85-88. Consider your health, family history, and lifestyle factors.
Example: Good health and family history suggests starting at 90
i Annual Aged Care Cost Total yearly cost for residential aged care including accommodation, care fees, and daily living expenses. Government subsidies reduce this, but you may still pay $40k-$100k+ annually.

How to Read This Analysis

Success Rate: Percentage of 1,000+ simulations where your money lasted through your expected lifespan. Higher is better. Arrows show improvement (↗) or decline (↘) vs Current Plan.

Median Balance: The middle result from Monte Carlo simulations at your expected lifespan age. This accounts for market volatility and represents the most likely outcome. Calculated using historical market returns and modern portfolio theory.

Risk Score: For Current Plan: Overall quality (0-100) based on success rate and balance adequacy. For alternatives: Relative performance vs Current Plan (50=same, >50=better, <50=worse).

Recommendation: Shows percentage improvements (+) or declines (-) in success rate and balance compared to your Current Plan baseline.

Aged Care Accommodation: RAD vs DAP

When entering a residential aged care facility, you can pay for your room as a lump sum RAD (Refundable Accommodation Deposit — refunded to your estate on exit) or as an ongoing daily charge DAP (Daily Accommodation Payment), or a combination.

How it works: The RAD is fully refundable — your estate gets it back when you leave the facility. The DAP is an ongoing daily charge calculated using the government's Maximum Permissible Interest Rate (MPIR, currently 7.21% pa). Paying the RAD in full avoids the DAP stream, but you lose the investment returns you could have earned on that money. If your expected portfolio return exceeds the MPIR, you may be better off paying DAP and investing the RAD funds.
About these projections: Figures are inflation-adjusted using category-specific rates (healthcare 6.5%, utilities 5%, general 2.9%). Aged care fees are based on 2024-25 government rates and include the basic daily fee (~$61/day), an average means-tested care fee, and a lifestyle extras package. Actual costs will vary by facility, care needs, and individual financial assessment. Home maintenance assumes $8,000/year for rates, insurance, and upkeep on a paid-off home. Travel and hobbies use your inputs from the calculator above.
