Personal & Risk Profile

i Your Current Age Enter your age today. This affects superannuation preservation rules, age pension eligibility, and asset allocation recommendations.
Example: If you're 49, you can access super at 60
i Partner's Current Age Your partner's age affects age pension calculations as couple rates apply when both are eligible. Leave blank if single.
Example: Younger partner delays full age pension benefits
i Your Retirement Age Age when you plan to stop working. Consider: Age pension starts at 67, but you can access super from preservation age (60-65 depending on birth year).
Example: Retire at 65, bridge gap until age pension at 67
i Your Expected Lifespan How long you expect to live. Australian life expectancy is ~81 for men, ~85 for women. Planning to 90+ ensures your money doesn't run out.
Example: Plan to 95 for conservative estimates

Risk Tolerance Assessment

i Risk Tolerance How comfortable you are with investment volatility. Conservative (1-3): Low growth, stable returns. Moderate (4-7): Balanced approach. Aggressive (8-10): High growth potential with higher volatility.

Current Finances

i Your Annual Salary (pre-tax) Gross income before tax and super. Used for tax calculations, super guarantee contributions (12%), and retirement projections.
Example: $100k salary = $12k super + $76k take-home (approx)

Example: $300k at age 50 could grow to $800k+ by retirement

Healthcare & Aged Care

i Aged Care Probability (%) Likelihood you'll need residential aged care. Australian statistics show ~70% of people will need aged care at some point. Consider family history, health, and gender (women have higher probability due to longer life expectancy).
Example: 68% means 68% chance of needing aged care costing ~$75k/year
i Expected Aged Care Start Age Average age when residential aged care begins. Australian average is around 85-88. Consider your health, family history, and lifestyle factors.
Example: Good health and family history suggests starting at 90
i Annual Aged Care Cost Total yearly cost for residential aged care including accommodation, care fees, and daily living expenses. Government subsidies reduce this, but you may still pay $40k-$100k+ annually.
Example: $76k/year includes accommodation payment + care fees

Economic & Asset Allocation

i Annual Inflation Rate (%) Expected long-term inflation rate. RBA targets 2-3% annually. Higher inflation erodes purchasing power, requiring larger retirement balances to maintain lifestyle.
Example: 3% inflation means $100 today = $74 purchasing power in 10 years
i Super Annual Growth (%) Expected annual return for your superannuation. Balanced super funds historically return 7-9% long-term. Growth options may achieve 9-11% but with higher volatility.
Example: 8% return doubles super balance roughly every 9 years

How to Read This Analysis

Success Rate: Percentage of 1,000+ simulations where your money lasted through your expected lifespan. Higher is better. Arrows show improvement (↗) or decline (↘) vs Current Plan.

Median Balance: The middle result from Monte Carlo simulations at your expected lifespan age. This accounts for market volatility and represents the most likely outcome. Calculated using historical market returns and modern portfolio theory.

Risk Score: For Current Plan: Overall quality (0-100) based on success rate and balance adequacy. For alternatives: Relative performance vs Current Plan (50=same, >50=better, <50=worse).

Recommendation: Shows percentage improvements (+) or declines (-) in success rate and balance compared to your Current Plan baseline.
