Personal & Risk Profile ⭐ Core Fields

i Your Current Age Enter your age today. This affects superannuation preservation rules, age pension eligibility, and asset allocation recommendations.
Example: If you're 49, you can access superannuation at 60
i Partner's Current Age Your partner's age affects age pension calculations as couple rates apply when both are eligible. Leave blank if single.
Example: Younger partner delays full age pension benefits
i Your Retirement Age Age when you plan to stop working. Consider: Age pension starts at 67, but you can access super from preservation age (60-65 depending on birth year).
Example: Retire at 65, bridge gap until age pension at 67
i Your Expected Lifespan How long you expect to live. Australian life expectancy is ~81 for men, ~85 for women. Planning to 90+ ensures your money doesn't run out.
Example: Plan to 95 for conservative estimates, or enter 0 to model until money runs out

Risk Tolerance Assessment

Current Finances

i Your Annual Salary (pre-tax) Gross income before tax and super. Used for tax calculations, super guarantee contributions (12%), and retirement projections.
Example: $100k salary = $12k super + $76k take-home (approx)
i Your Current Superannuation Total super balance today across all funds. Check your myGov account or latest super statement for accurate figures.
Example: $300k at age 50 could grow to $800k+ by retirement
i Current Cash/Savings Bank accounts, term deposits, cash investments. Include emergency fund and any cash earmarked for retirement.
Example: $50k emergency fund + $75k investment cash = $125k
i % of Post-Tax Income Saved Percentage of after-tax income you save for retirement (excluding super). Higher savings rates dramatically improve retirement outcomes.
Example: Save 10% of $80k take-home = $8k annually

Property Portfolio Optional

Primary Residence

i Current Home Value Today's market value of your primary residence. Use recent sales of comparable properties or professional valuation. Home value affects age pension asset test but is partially exempt.
Example: $800k home may add ~$200k to age pension assets test
i Plan to Downsize? Whether you plan to sell and buy a smaller/cheaper home in retirement. Downsizing can free up capital and reduce living costs. The downsizer contribution allows up to $300k into super.
Example: $1.2M home → $800k apartment = $400k freed up

Economic & Asset Allocation 💡 Advanced

i Annual Inflation Rate (%) Expected long-term inflation rate. RBA targets 2-3% annually. Higher inflation erodes purchasing power, requiring larger retirement balances to maintain lifestyle.
Example: 3% inflation means $100 today = $74 purchasing power in 10 years
i Super Annual Growth (%) Expected annual return for your superannuation. Balanced super funds historically return 7-9% long-term. Growth options may achieve 9-11% but with higher volatility.
Example: 8% return doubles super balance roughly every 9 years
